# FinEase
💵 FinEase - A Family Budgeting and Finance App

FinEase is a AI powered desktop app designed for families to track their spending, set shared goals and make smarter decisions with their money. Get personalized insights, automated budgets and real time advice with the AI assistant tailored to your needs specifically.

💡 Why we built this ?

Financial strain is a concern for many families. Our app gives households a clear picture of their income and spending, helping avoid mismanagement of money and debt. It encourages mindful spending and creates a safety net incase of unexpected emergencies.
